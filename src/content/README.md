## Draft conventions

- `[TBC: ...]` marks something only you can confirm or decide. Nothing on
  the site goes live while a `[TBC` remains.
- `# SOURCE:` comments record where a fact was checked, so it can be
  re-checked later.
- Quotes from other people are copied verbatim from the live page on the
  date noted. Don't paraphrase them.
